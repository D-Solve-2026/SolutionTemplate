# Pitch Deck Outline — [Project Name]

Prepare slides to accompany your **3–5 minute live demo**. No rigid format is
enforced — this outline is a safe starting point. Total runtime ≈ 4 minutes.

---

## Slide 1 — Title (≈30s)

- Project name + tagline
- Team name & members, college
- Problem number/name

## Slide 2 — The Problem (≈45s)

- The pain point (use real numbers if you found any: e.g. "20–30 min per patient
  for insurance verification")
- Who feels it (patients, practices, clinical teams, insurers)

## Slide 3 — The Solution (≈45s)

- Your product in one sentence
- How it works (simple 3-step flow)
- Key differentiator vs. status quo

## Slide 4 — Live Demo (≈90–120s)

- Show the working prototype, not just slides
- Narrate what's happening on screen
- End on the "wow" moment

## Slide 5 — Tech Highlights (≈30s)

- Stack summary
- The one technically interesting thing you'd love to be asked about

## Slide 6 — Market & Impact (≈30s)

- Target users
- Why it matters for the dental industry (UK/US/AU focus for Problem 6)

## Slide 7 — Roadmap & Wrap-up (≈30s)

- What's next if you had more time
- Thank you + one-line takeaway

---

## Q&A Preparation (5–10 min)

Have every team member ready to answer:

1. Why did you pick this stack / design?
2. How does [the AI/model/algorithm] actually work?
3. What fails, and how do you handle it?
4. How does this differ from existing products (e.g. on G2/Capterra)?
5. What did you try that didn't work?
6. Privacy/security: how is patient data protected?
